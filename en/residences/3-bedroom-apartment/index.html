Questions about this category

What is the starting price?

The supplied brochure lists 3-bedroom apartments from AED 4,999,000 and USD 1,360,272. These are brochure figures, not a current availability guarantee. Obtain a written unit-specific offer.

What floor plan is available?

Two three-bedroom layouts are illustrated: Type 1 totals 121.91 m² and Type 2 totals 111.29 m². Their bedroom arrangements and balcony areas differ. The supplied plan gives a concrete basis for reviewing circulation and outdoor space before requesting an available unit.

Can I buy this category from abroad?

Remote purchase depends on the developer's process and the unit offered. A passport begins the file; additional identity, address or source-of-funds documents may be required.

Does this price guarantee a Golden Visa?

No. A price above AED 2 million does not itself guarantee residency. Golden Visa eligibility depends on the qualifying investment and official conditions; retail and residential purchases should be assessed individually.
