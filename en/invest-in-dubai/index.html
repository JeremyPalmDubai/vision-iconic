05 / FAQ

GOOD QUESTIONS.
CLEAR ANSWERS.

What is Vision Iconic?

Vision Iconic is a residential and retail tower at Mercedes-Benz Places | Binghatti City in Nad Al Sheba 1, Dubai. The supplied brochure describes 67 residential floors and 1,426 apartments.

What are the starting prices in AED and USD?

The brochure lists one-bedroom residences from AED 2,250,000 and USD 612,245. Prices depend on the residence and release. Request a current written quotation before making a decision.

Can I buy remotely with only a passport?

Remote buying may be possible. A passport is a starting document, not a guarantee that no other documents will be needed. The developer, bank and authorities may require identity, address and source-of-funds checks.

Are salary slips required for the payment plan?

A developer payment plan is different from mortgage finance and may not require salary slips. The actual requirements and schedule must be confirmed with the developer; compliance documents may still be requested.

Is Dubai property completely tax-free?

The UAE does not levy personal income tax, and qualifying personal property investment income and gains are generally excluded from UAE corporate tax. Registration fees and other property costs apply. Your home country may impose additional taxes.

Does buying a residence guarantee a Golden Visa?

No. A qualifying property investment of at least AED 2 million can provide a route to a real estate Golden Visa, subject to official conditions and approval. Other property investor residence options have separate criteria.

Is agency commission really zero?

A zero buyer-side commission offer needs written confirmation from the agency. It does not remove government registration fees, developer charges, service charges or other purchase costs.
