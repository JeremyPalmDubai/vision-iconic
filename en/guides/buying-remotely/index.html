Project/Residences at Vision Iconic/Buying Vision Iconic remotely from abroad

Information checked against the supplied brochure · 5 October 2026

What the source confirms

Begin with the exact residence: unit number, category, floor plan, price and contractual terms. Use the residence pages to compare the supplied layouts, then request the actual offered unit plan. Project renders show the architectural vision; they do not establish the condition or view of every individual unit.

Conditions to verify

Remote completion may be supported by the developer, but the process must be confirmed. Establish how identification, signing, payments and registration will be handled. A passport is a starting document. Identity, address and source-of-funds checks can still be required by the parties involved.

Before you decide

Before transferring funds, verify the developer and project information through Dubai Land Department, review the sale documents and confirm the authorised payment instructions. Overseas buyers should also check the reporting and tax consequences in their country of residence. Do not treat a simplified sales process as an exemption from compliance checks.

What to confirm before buying

  • Current written price and exact unit number
  • Developer payment schedule and total purchase costs
  • Floor plan, included features and contractual terms
  • Registration process and any visa conditions
