Project/Residences at Vision Iconic/Dubai property tax and purchase costs

Information checked against the supplied brochure · 5 October 2026

What the source confirms

The UAE does not levy personal income tax. Federal Tax Authority guidance generally excludes qualifying personal real estate investment income from corporate tax. The conditions and whether an activity is personal investment or a licensed business matter; a broad 0% message is not a substitute for checking your own circumstances.

Conditions to verify

Your country of tax residence may impose reporting, income or capital-gains obligations even when the UAE treatment is favourable. A residence visa does not automatically determine tax residence. Overseas buyers should obtain advice relevant to their own country rather than assuming that a Dubai purchase eliminates worldwide taxation.

Before you decide

Government registration fees and other property costs still apply. A zero buyer-side agency commission offer should be confirmed in writing and does not eliminate developer charges, registration costs or recurring service charges. Request an itemised purchase budget and the expected ongoing charges before comparing investment options.

What to confirm before buying

  • Current written price and exact unit number
  • Developer payment schedule and total purchase costs
  • Floor plan, included features and contractual terms
  • Registration process and any visa conditions
