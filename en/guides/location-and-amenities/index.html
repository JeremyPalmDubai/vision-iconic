Project/Residences at Vision Iconic/Vision Iconic location and amenities in Dubai

Information checked against the supplied brochure · 5 October 2026

What the source confirms

The supplied brochure locates Vision Iconic in Nad Al Sheba 1 within Mercedes-Benz Places | Binghatti City. It identifies the surrounding district with Meydan's equestrian setting and racecourse. No verified travel-time table is supplied, so journey times should be checked for your actual destinations and traffic conditions.

Conditions to verify

Vision Iconic is described as a mixed residential and retail tower with 67 residential floors, 1,426 apartments and 27 ground-floor shops. The architectural language includes a terraced silver spine, a circular podium and a 2,966 m² water plaza. These are project-level features, not specifications for an individual apartment.

Before you decide

The brochure lists sky and podium pools, concierge and valet services, spa facilities, fitness, yoga and smart systems. Confirm access, operating arrangements, handover specification and service charges in the actual sale documents. The brochure describes a LEED qualification ambition, not a completed certification.

What to confirm before buying

  • Current written price and exact unit number
  • Developer payment schedule and total purchase costs
  • Floor plan, included features and contractual terms
  • Registration process and any visa conditions
